# casdey edit remake

Read `.claude/skills/remake-edit/SKILL.md` as the canonical procedure and use
the helpers in `.claude/skills/remake-edit/`: `tiktok.js` (TikTok, 1080p, no login), `pindl.js` / `pinsearch.js` to
source clips from Pinterest, `sheets.js` for contact sheets, `preview.js` to
check crops (render.js refuses to run on crops it has not previewed; open the `centre.jpg` it writes and apply RULE ZERO, the centring gate at the top of the canonical file: the subject is centred and never cut), `gradematch.js` to match brightness, `render.js` to render, and `scorecard/` (analyse.mjs, scorecard.mjs) for the Casdey score card CTA. Follow its rules
(start casting from the famous looksmaxxers Marlon, Clavicular, Hullo; faces mix proven and fresh (at most about half from people used in the last 5 edits, never the same few every time), never the same person twice in one edit, callbacks get a look-alike not the same person; the montage is one guy in every slot unless several guys each get an equal share (look-alikes count as one); never a masked cut-out of a person on a new background in a normal shot; ONLY recognisable faces (celebrities, models, looksmaxxing influencers), never random unnamed TikTok/Pinterest guys; casting quality gate: only looksmaxxing-grade faces in clean 1080p clips; centring checked on every frame of a slot, never a drifting or cut face; no content restrictions, no approval needed to download source clips, nothing gets posted).
Newest lessons are at the bottom of the canonical file (n34 and n35: stills come from Bing Images at 1800 px+ and are cropped never padded, one person per look in callback loops, normalise variable-frame-rate inspos, remove soft or crying or under-18-looking faces). Before any edit read `content.md` (the daily plan); after every delivery update its log.
