# casdey content plan: Instagram, 100 days

Decided 2026-09-13 with Davide, reasoned through `/hormozi`. The ledger entry is
"2026-09-13, The founder's idle week" in `.claude/skills/hormozi/references/casdey-ledger.md`.
This plan adds a channel. It does not touch the outreach routines, the T2 test or the
send volume.

---

## The decisions

| | |
| --- | --- |
| Platform | Instagram only, on casdey's existing account (the one the cold DMs come from) |
| Format | Faceless for now: no face, no voice. Carousels, text graphics, captioned screen recordings of casdey, cuts of the promo film |
| Cadence | 1 post a day for 100 days. First post Wednesday 2026-09-16, day 100 is 2026-12-24 |
| Language | English |
| Audience | Owners of independent gyms, CrossFit and community boxes, and studios in Ireland, the UK and Europe |
| Topic | Members who leave, and getting them back. Nothing broader until the 100 days are done |

## Why this, in Hormozi's terms

- **More time than money.** "If I have more time than money, I move to posting content"
  (*$100M Leads* pg 245). That is Davide's situation exactly.
- **It makes the cold channel work better.** "If you reach out to someone and they can't
  find content related to your services, they're less likely to buy" (pg 114). casdey's
  Instagram DMs stand at 105 sent, 0 replies, from a thin profile.
- **King of the puddle first.** Narrow the topic to exactly what you do and for whom
  (pg 143). casdey's puddle is gym owners and their ex-members, not "fitness business" and
  not "marketing".
- **His 2026 advice, from third-party summaries of his videos** (sources at the bottom):
  one narrow audience, education over entertainment, treat every viewer as a stranger who
  has never heard of you, and judge content on quality signals (opt-ins, enquiries), not
  views.
- **Do. Not. Stop.** Pick a cadence you can hold, then keep it (pg 145). Hormozi posted a
  podcast twice a week for four years before it charted. A burst that dies when the week
  gets busy is worth nothing.

**Where this deviates:** Hormozi's content is face-led, and a face carries Likeness, one of
the four things his 2026 material says builds influence. Going faceless costs trust.
Accepted for now, on Davide's call; revisited at the day 30 review.

## What the posts are about: five pillars

Each maps to one of Hormozi's topic types (pg 117-119).

| # | Pillar | Hormozi type | Per week | Example |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | **Founder log.** Real numbers from casdey's own outreach and build, told as "how I", never "how to" | Recent past | 1 | "I emailed 926 gym owners about their ex-members. 12 replied. Here's what they said." |
| 2 | **The win-back playbook, given away.** How to win back a cancelled member by hand: who to contact first, when, what to say, what not to offer, the actual message | Education | 3 | "The 4-line message that brings a cancelled member back. Save this." |
| 3 | **The cost of doing nothing.** Simple maths the owner runs on their own numbers | Reveal the problem | 1 | "Count the members who cancelled this year. Multiply by your monthly price. That's the number." |
| 4 | **casdey at work.** Screen recordings: the lapsed list, the message it writes, the booking step | Sample of the service | 1 | "What casdey sends a member who left 3 months ago, in the gym's own name." |
| 5 | **Proof.** A real gym's result, only with their permission | Recent past, case study | when it exists | BodyActive, once there is a result. Takes pillar 3's slot |

That is 7 a week. Pillar 2 is the backbone, and it follows the lead-magnet rule
"give away the secrets, sell the implementation" (*$100M Leads*, lead magnets step 6): a gym
owner who learns the whole method still has no time to do it for 200 members, and that is what
casdey sells.

**Hard rules for every post:**
- Never an invented statistic. Cite a real source on the slide, or use the owner's own numbers.
- Never a fake customer, fake gym or fake result.
- "casdey" always lowercase. No em dashes. Brand v4 (Chalk ground, Leaf gold, Outfit titles, IBM Plex body), per `brand assets/casdey-brand-guide.html`.

## Give and ask

- **Intermittent asks** (pg 135-136): on short-form platforms, many pure give posts, then one
  ask. Here that means **6 gives, 1 ask, every week**.
- **The ask is the lead magnet, not the product** (pg 136: "if you're not sure, do the lead
  magnet"). It is the same give as the T2 email: *"DM me VIDEO and I'll send you a 2-minute
  video, plus the exact message casdey would send your ex-members."*
- **One call to action per post** (pg 141). On give posts that is "save this" or "send this to
  whoever handles your memberships" (shares by DM are Instagram's strongest distribution signal
  in 2026, per the carousel sources below). On the ask post it is the DM keyword, nothing else.
- A "VIDEO" DM is an **engaged lead** and counts toward the weekly 1% goal. Deliver the video
  and the gym's message the same day.

## Anatomy of a carousel

1. **Slide 1, the hook:** the promise, specific, in few words (hook, retain, reward, pg 115).
2. **Slide 2, who is talking:** one line, e.g. "I'm Davide. I build casdey, and I've emailed
   926 gym owners about their ex-members." Assume the viewer has never heard of casdey.
3. **Slides 3 to 7, the reward:** one idea per slide.
4. **Last slide:** the one CTA.

## The 100 days

### Week 0, 2026-09-14 to 2026-09-15: set up
- [ ] **Profile.** Mark as the avatar. Bio in three lines: who it is for, the result, the
      action ("DM VIDEO"). Link to casdey.com.
- [ ] **Three pinned posts** before anything else goes out: what casdey does (a cut of the
      promo film), founder log #1, one playbook post. A stranger opening the profile from a DM
      should understand casdey in ten seconds.
- [ ] **Batch 1:** posts for days 1 to 15.
- [ ] **Decide how VIDEO DMs get logged**, before the first ask post.

### Days 1 to 30, 2026-09-16 to 2026-10-15: hold the inputs
- **Post by hand every day**, not pre-scheduled. Hormozi finds hand-posted content performs
  better because the instant feedback makes you try harder (pg 144, lesson 7). About 10
  minutes, including replying to comments, next to the daily DMs.
- **Batch twice a month**, which is Hormozi's own routine (pg 140): a batch around
  2026-09-15 and 2026-09-30.
- **Test formats, never topics:** carousel against captioned reel against single graphic,
  inside the same pillars.
- Change nothing else before day 30.

### Day 30 review, 2026-10-15
Numbers below. Decide: which format to do more of, whether to revisit face or voice, and
whether AI UGC gets a test.

### Days 31 to 60, review 2026-11-14
Same cadence, more of what worked. Pillar 5 takes over as soon as a real result exists.

### Days 61 to 100, review 2026-12-24
The only point where stopping, or changing platform, is on the table.

## What gets measured

Measured monthly (pg 145), never judged on one post or one week.

| Type | Measure | Target or baseline |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Input | Posts published | 7 a week, every week |
| Size | Followers and accounts reached | Absolute, and growth % month on month |
| Quality | VIDEO DMs, per week and per 1,000 accounts reached | Baseline set in month 1 |
| Knock-on | Cold DM reply rate | 0 from 105 before the profile had content |

## AI UGC: parked, notes for when it comes up

- **Instagram's rule since 2026-08-31:** a profile that features an AI-generated person must
  carry the "AI-generated profile" label, or it loses reach to people who don't already follow
  it. AI tools used on content about real things do not need it; a synthetic person does.
  Check exactly how that applies to a brand account posting the occasional AI presenter
  before using one.
- **Never an AI "gym owner" or "customer" saying casdey worked.** That is manufactured social
  proof, banned across all casdey outreach, and fake credibility wrecks trust the moment it is
  spotted.
- A disclosed AI presenter *explaining* how casdey works is a fair test for the day 30 review.
