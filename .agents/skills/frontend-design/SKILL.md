# casdey frontend design

Read `.claude/skills/frontend-design/SKILL.md` and
`brand assets/CLAUDE_DESIGN.md` before frontend or visual work. The brand guide
is the visual source of truth.

Follow the design principles and review intent, but use the repository's
current tooling and scripts rather than blindly following stale paths or
single-file defaults in the historical design document. Do not invent visual
assets or alter the brand without an explicit request.
